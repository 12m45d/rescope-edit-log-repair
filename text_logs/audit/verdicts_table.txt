RESIDUAL RISKS
  - Reproducibility is established on this machine and stack only (RTX 5090, torch 2.8.0, transformers 4.23.1): the public environment (torch 1.10.2, transformers 4.15.0) cannot be installed for Python 3.11, so what the authors' stack would write bit for bit is untested; the tie to the paper is the single-edit scores (0.0 / 0.3 / 0.0 points) and the unedited-model scores.
  - One stream, one draw of the public code's 21 random context templates (seed 0).  With another draw (2 of 21 templates equal) the single-edit factors of the same 150 records differ by 30 % (median, output-side factor) while ES / PS / NS move by 0.0 / 0.0 / -0.07 points; the variance of sequential-log metrics over template draws is unknown.
  - The development log has no request whose subject is the first prompt token and no exploding update (base rate 80 of 21919 records).  In the library format the read-out vectors of such updates have norm up to 57.3 against a median of 0.10 (measured).  What that does to the method's scores on these logs was not measured (discipline); nothing developed on the development log has met such an update.
  - Records are disjoint, prompt texts are not: 310 of the 1000 neighbourhood rows of the development log are also prompts of the reported stream (305 distinct texts; 42 rows of T100, 622 of T2000); 22 HELD-OUT benign prompts are neighbourhood prompts of logged records and 4 are logged requests (flags exist: use the 'clean' rows).
  - D1 on 10-token continuations is close to its ceiling on the logs that did NOT collapse (dev100 95.4 %, T100 93.5 %; audit after 1 / 10 updates: 6.0 / 28.0 %); the share of tokens kept (64.7 / 69.8 %) and of changed first tokens (38.3 / 33.2 %) are further from their ceiling.
  - The public n-gram entropy (metric v) did not separate the collapsed T500 / T1000 from the non-collapsed dev100: 578.6 / 587.3 against 587.2 (pre-edit 626.3), while 8 words make up 60 % of the generated words (T2000: 503.1).  For scale, the public formula on uniformly random sequences of the same length (90 word tokens) gives 539 for 5 symbols and 616 for 10.
  - Requests that repeat a subject / a whole request prompt inside a log (5 of 5 such prompt pairs of the stream have bitwise identical solver keys and 5 different new objects): T500 2 / 0, T1000 4 / 1, T2000 21 / 5; none in dev100 and T100.
  - Float32: at T2000 the deployed weight has entries up to 535; the exact (fp64) sum of the logged terms differs from it by up to 8.7e-04 per entry, so a replay in another precision is the deployed model only up to that round-off (float32 vs float64 forward at T1000: same argmax on 40 of 40 prompts).
  - The registered behavioural evaluation of E10 (amendment R19 f) uses the logs with 500 and 2000 requests: both are collapse logs; the only clean reported log (T100) is registered at gate level only.
  - Checkpoint and tokenizer were verified against the listing of hf-mirror.com (huggingface.co is not reachable from this machine).
